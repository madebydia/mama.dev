# Take-Away Train

The subtraction sibling of Add-Up Train, built with [Three.js](https://threejs.org/). At misty dawn, a maroon steam engine pulls one wagon of apples — some hop out onto the meadow ("5 apples… 2 hop away!") and the child answers `5 − 2 = ?` by counting what's left. Choices on wooden tiles; the train chugs off on success.

Levels: within 5 → within 10 → bigger take-aways. Streak meter, level picker, spoken equations.
