# Add-Up Train

A 3D addition game built with [Three.js](https://threejs.org/). A freight train rolls up at sunset pulling two open wagons of apples — one with `a`, one with `b`. How many apples altogether? Pick the sum and the train chugs off to the finish. Builds early addition and the link between counting real things and the `+` symbol.

Four levels grow from sums to 6 up to sums to 20, with a streak meter and a level picker. Styled as a warm desert sunset to feel distinct from the Word Train spelling game.

**Play:** [mama.dev/games/add-up-train](https://mama.dev/games/add-up-train/)
