# What to eat?

The menu is driven by `foods.json`. To add a food, copy an existing record, give it a unique lowercase `id`, choose a section and color, add its toddler portion and nutrition, then place a square WebP illustration in `images/` and set `image` to its relative path.

Illustrations should show the food exactly as served, using a warm cream plate or bowl only when the food needs one, in a softly textured gouache/watercolor style. Use a centered three-quarter view, gentle natural shadows, no utensils, garnish, faces, text, border, or scenery. Export at 768 × 768 pixels with genuine alpha transparency, fully transparent corners, and a clean anti-aliased edge without a white fringe, gray halo, checkerboard, or baked background.
